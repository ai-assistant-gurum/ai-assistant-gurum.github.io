Article 1 (Purpose)

These Terms set out the rights, obligations, and responsibilities of the Montie developer (individual) (the "Operator") and Users regarding services that use location information (the "Location-Based Services") in the Montie app (the "App") provided by the Operator.

Article 2 (Matters Not Covered by These Terms)

Matters not provided for in these Terms are governed by applicable laws such as the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information (위치정보의 보호 및 이용 등에 관한 법률) and the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법), and by Montie's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Article 3 (Content of the Service and Fees)

If the User has granted the location permission, Montie uses the location of the User's device for the following features.

  • "Current location" on the schedule screen: displaying the city, time, and weather where the User is now
  • Weather questions and briefings: the weather forecast for where the User is now
  • Assistant requests: when the User requests something that requires location, Montie checks the current location (address, coordinates) and handles the request

The Location-Based Services are free of charge. However, data communication fees follow the fee policy of the carrier the User subscribes to.

Article 4 (How Personal Location Information Is Processed)

  • Montie uses the User's personal location information only on the User's device and does not transmit it to the Operator's servers or location information systems. The Operator does not collect, store, or provide the User's personal location information.
  • To retrieve the weather, Montie sends only location coordinates rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) directly to a public weather service (Open-Meteo). This request contains no information that identifies the User.
  • When the User asks the assistant to do something that requires location, Montie sends the current location (address, coordinates) to the AI service the User has connected as part of the conversation content. This transmission is made directly to the service the User has connected, at the User's request, and the content sent can be viewed in "Settings › AI connections › What was sent to AI".
  • Montie does not continuously track location in the background. It reads the location only while the App is on screen or when the User requests it.

Article 5 (Retention Period of Personal Location Information)

  • The last location used for the "Current location" display is stored on the User's device and is replaced each time a new location is read (about every 30 minutes).
  • Weather results are kept briefly on the device.
  • A location contained in an assistant conversation remains on the device until the User deletes that conversation.
  • All of the above is deleted when the User deletes it, performs "Settings › General › Reset", or uninstalls the App.

Article 6 (Records Confirming Use and Provision of Location Information)

Because the Operator does not collect or provide the User's personal location information, the Operator holds no records confirming the use or provision of location information. A location sent to an AI service the User has connected can be checked for 30 days in the "What was sent to AI" record on the User's device.

Article 7 (Rights of the Subject of Personal Location Information)

  • The User may withdraw all or part of the consent to the use of location at any time. To do so, withdraw Montie's location permission in the smartphone settings, or turn off "Also show where I am" on the schedule screen.
  • The User may at any time request a temporary suspension of the use of location, and may exercise this right by changing the location permission to "Only this time" or "Don't allow".
  • The User can directly view and delete location-related records remaining on the device. The User may also request access, notification, or correction from the Operator, but the Operator does not hold the User's personal location information.

Article 8 (Rights of Legal Guardians)

Because Montie may be used only by persons aged 14 or older, the Operator does not provide a service that uses the personal location information of children under 14. A legal guardian (such as a parent) who learns that a child under 14 is using Montie may withdraw the location permission on the child's device or uninstall the app, and may exercise the User's rights under these Terms.

Article 9 (Rights of Guardians of Children Aged 8 or Under, etc.)

When a guardian of a child aged 8 or under, a person under adult guardianship, or a person with a severe disability consents to the use of personal location information in order to protect that person's life or body, the consent of the person concerned is deemed to have been given. The guardian may exercise all of the rights of the subject of personal location information under these Terms.

Article 10 (Provision of Personal Location Information to Third Parties)

The Operator does not provide the User's personal location information to third parties. The transmissions to the weather service in Article 4 and to the connected AI service are made directly by the User's device according to the User's requests or settings, and the User can check their content on the device.
