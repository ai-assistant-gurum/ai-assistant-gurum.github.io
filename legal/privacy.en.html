Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean original, the Korean original prevails.

The Gurum developer (individual) (the "Operator") has established this Privacy Policy to protect the personal information of people who use the Gurum app (the "App") (the "Users") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) and other applicable laws.

1. How Gurum Handles Personal Information — On the User's Device

Gurum runs inside the User's smartphone, without any server operated by the Operator. Information such as conversations, memories, schedules, the password vault, and the collection of text messages and notifications is all stored on the User's device and processed on the device.

  • The Operator does not require sign-up and does not receive or store the User's information, such as name, phone number, email, conversation content, or location, on the Operator's servers.
  • Gurum does not use advertising, usage analytics, or crash reporting tools.
  • The User's information leaves the device only when the App sends it directly to a service the User has connected (such as an AI service or a Google account) or to the public services listed in Section 4 of this policy. This information does not pass through the Operator.

2. Personal Information Processed by the Operator

The Operator does not receive or store any personal information of Users. No information needs to be sent to the Operator in order to use Gurum, and the distribution address managed by the Operator does not keep access logs either.

If a User contacts the Operator by email, the Operator processes the email address and the content of the inquiry in order to reply, and deletes them without delay once the inquiry is resolved.

3. Information Processed Only on the Device

The following information is processed on the User's device, for the relevant feature only, and only when the User turns on the feature or grants the permission. It is not sent to the Operator.

  • Conversations, memories, schedules, to-dos, and the User's own information entered by the User (name, phone number, address, etc.): assistant features
  • Password vault (ID, password, and one-time authentication code settings): password management and autofill
  • Location (approximate or precise): displaying the current time and weather where the User is, and handling User requests that require location. For details, the Location-Based Services Terms of Use apply.
  • Text messages, call logs, notifications (from apps the User selects), contacts, and calendar: viewing and sorting received messages in one place, entering verification codes, smishing and important-contact alerts, subscription payment alerts, and schedule management
  • Microphone audio: voice conversation and the wake word (wake word recognition is processed on the device)
  • Screen content of other apps (accessibility service): operating other apps as requested by the User
  • List and permissions of installed apps, app usage statistics, and files in the Downloads folder: diagnosing my smartphone
  • Browsing history and login state (cookies) of the in-app browser: web tasks requested by the User

This information remains on the device until the User deletes it, resets the App, or uninstalls the App.

4. Information Sent Off the Device

Gurum sends information directly to the following services according to the User's requests or settings. Because these transmissions do not pass through the Operator, they do not constitute provision of personal information to a third party or entrustment of processing by the Operator, and the privacy policy and terms of the receiving service apply.

  • AI services connected by the User (the User's account or API key): conversation content, attached files and photos, and information needed for tasks the User requests (for example, schedules, email and text message content, location, and the content and screenshots of other apps' screens). If a fast-decision AI is connected, newly received text messages and notifications may be sent to it for classification. The content Gurum has sent to AI services can be viewed directly for 30 days in "Settings › AI settings › What was sent to AI".
  • Voice and image services connected by the User: voice and request content when using voice conversation or image creation
  • Google account (if connected by the User): reading, sending, and organizing email (Gmail), storing encrypted backup files (the app-specific space of Google Drive), and storing the vault recovery key (Google Block Store)
  • Weather service (Open-Meteo): a city name or location coordinates to retrieve the weather. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) before being sent.
  • Password breach check (if turned on by the User): only the first 5 characters of the hash of the password are sent; the password itself is never sent.
  • Public Data Portal (if the User has registered a key): the query conditions requested by the User
  • In-app browser and web search: general web connection information sent to the sites the User visits or searches
  • Address search: the search term sent to the postal code search service when entering an address
  • Downloading updates, instructions (skills), and the AI service list: file requests to public distribution hosting (Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, jsDelivr) and to the Operator's backup distribution address. General access logs (such as the IP address) may remain with the public hosting providers. The Operator's backup distribution address keeps no access logs, and no user data is sent.

If a service connected by the User is located overseas, the above information may be transferred to that country at the User's request.

5. Provision to Third Parties and Entrustment of Processing

The Operator does not provide the User's personal information to third parties and does not entrust the processing of personal information.

6. Use of Generative AI and the User's Choices

  • Gurum acts as the User's assistant (conversation, organizing, managing schedules, email, and text messages, operating other apps and the web, etc.) using the AI services the User has connected directly.
  • The Operator does not collect what the User enters or the results the AI produces, and does not use them for AI training. The Operator is not involved in the processing between the User and the AI services the User has connected.
  • Whether content sent to an AI service connected by the User is stored by that service or used for AI training depends on that service's terms and settings. Which AI service to connect and whether to adjust settings such as opting out of training are the User's choice, and the responsibility for that also lies with the User.
  • The content Gurum has sent to AI services can be viewed directly for 30 days in "Settings › AI settings › What was sent to AI". This record exists only on the device and is automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Conversations and results remain on the User's device until the User deletes them.
  • AI answers may be wrong. For actions that cannot be undone, such as payment, deletion, and sending, Gurum asks for the User's confirmation before carrying them out.

7. User's Choices and Responsibility

The Operator does not run a server that receives the User's information, and Gurum operates on the User's device according to the User's instructions and settings. The User decides which permissions to grant, which services to connect, and what information to send to AI services, and the processing and results of that information are a matter between the User and the services the User has connected. Managing backups, the master password, the recovery key, and the device is also the User's responsibility. The Operator's responsibility and its limits are governed by the Terms of Service.

8. Destruction of Personal Information

  • Information on the device: It is deleted immediately when the User deletes conversations, memories, or records. If the User performs "Settings › General › Reset", all Gurum data on this device is deleted, and, if selected, the cloud backup and the vault recovery key are deleted as well. Uninstalling the App also deletes the data on the device.
  • The Operator does not hold any User information.

9. User's Rights and How to Exercise Them

Users may at any time access, correct, and delete their personal information and request suspension of processing. Because Gurum's information is on the User's device, the User can exercise these rights directly within the App.

  • Access and correction: View and edit conversations, memories, schedules, the vault, and my information on each screen. View what was sent to AI services in "What was sent to AI".
  • Deletion: Delete each item or perform "Reset".
  • Suspension of processing and withdrawal of consent: Turn off the feature switch, withdraw the permission in the smartphone settings, or disconnect the connected service (AI, Google account).
  • Inquiries and requests concerning personal information can be made through the contact below, and the Operator will handle them without delay.

10. Children Under 14

The Operator does not collect any personal information, including the User's age, and therefore does not collect personal information of children under 14. When a child under 14 uses Gurum, please have a legal guardian (such as a parent) set it up together and supervise. The AI services and Google accounts the User connects follow each service's age requirements.

11. Security Measures

  • The password vault is encrypted with a key wrapped by the User's master password, recovery key, and device security key.
  • AI service keys and connection information are sealed with the smartphone's secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • Backup files are stored only in encrypted form, and the Operator cannot open backups.
  • The computer connection exchanges data within the same Wi-Fi network using end-to-end encryption (Noise protocol) and does not pass through the Operator's servers.
  • App data is prevented from leaving the device through Android's own cloud backup.
  • An app lock (screen lock, biometric authentication) can be set.
  • Updates and instruction files are applied only after verifying the Operator's digital signature.

12. Automatic Collection Tools

The Operator does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics tools. Cookies from sites visited in the in-app browser are stored only on the User's device and are subject to the policies of those sites.

13. Automated Decisions

The Operator does not make automated decisions about Users. AI features that the User has turned on, such as classifying text messages and notifications, are processed on the User's device and by the AI service the User has connected, and the User can turn these features off at any time.

16. Users Outside Korea

Gurum is distributed worldwide. The Operator does not collect, sell, or share the User's personal information and is not a business or controller that processes the User's personal information.

  • Users in the EU and UK (GDPR): The rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and objection can be exercised by directly viewing, exporting (as a backup file), or deleting the data on the device. Please send inquiries to the email above; you may also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.
  • Users in California, U.S. (CCPA/CPRA) and similar jurisdictions: The Operator does not sell or share (for cross-context behavioral advertising) personal information. The rights to know, delete, and correct can be exercised directly on the device, and the Operator does not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.
  • Information sent to services connected by the User is processed according to the policies of those services.

17. Changes to This Policy

When this policy is changed, notice will be given through an app update notice and a public web page starting 7 days before the effective date (30 days before for changes that are unfavorable to Users), and a comparison of the content before and after the change will be provided.
